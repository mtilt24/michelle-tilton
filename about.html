Get to know me

I was tired of watching good organizations get bad marketing.

For 15+ years I've led marketing at B2B technology companies. The work I find most meaningful is helping small businesses and nonprofits get the strategy they actually deserve.

I'm deeply passionate about digital marketing, especially when it comes to empowering small businesses. Understanding the challenges that entrepreneurs face in the evolving digital world is key, and I'm dedicated to offering guidance through the intricate landscape of online marketing.

I hold a Bachelor's Degree in Business and Marketing from Sacramento State and a Master's Degree in Communications and Digital Strategy from the University of Florida.

Professionally, I serve as the VP of Marketing at a telecommunications company. Previously, I've brought my expertise to roles like Digital Marketing Specialist and Social Media Marketing Specialist and even took the reins as the VP of Communications for a non-profit.

Small businesses and nonprofits don't need another agency that treats them like an afterthought. You need someone who brings senior-level strategy, actually does the work, and charges like a human.
