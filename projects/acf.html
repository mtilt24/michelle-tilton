The work

A brand from zero, then a site to put it on.

Two founders walked in ready to support foster youth aging out of the system, with no website, no logo, and no brand. They needed something that could walk into donor meetings, hold its own next to bigger nonprofits, and turn curious visitors into people who actually give.

The work started from scratch: logo, color system, typography, voice, and photography direction. From there, a custom-coded multi-page website with Stripe-powered donations and event ticketing, live the day the site launched, in time for the May fundraiser.
