Client
Advancing Communities Forward (ACF Inc.)
Industry
501(c)(3) nonprofit supporting foster youth ages 18 to 21
Problem
Two passionate founders and a tax ID, but no website, no brand identity, and a fundraising event already on the calendar.
What I did
Brand identity, logo design, copywriting, and a custom-coded multi-page site with live donation and ticket flows.
Result
Inquiries tripled in 30 days. The site makes ACF feel like the established nonprofit it's becoming.
What I built

Every choice, made on purpose.

A brand built from scratch

They came with a tax ID and a mission. I designed the logo, color palette, typography, and voice from zero, so the founders could walk into donor and partner meetings with a real visual identity behind them.

A site that does the talking when the founders can't

Seven custom-coded pages built around one editorial rule: lead with outcomes for the youth, not the trauma they came from. Every photo, headline, and CTA points the visitor toward how they can help.

Donations live the day it launched

Stripe integrated directly into the site for both donations and event tickets. No third-party donation platforms taking a cut, no extra accounts to manage. The May fundraiser ran through it without a hitch.

Built lean, built to last

Hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JS hosted on Netlify with images served through Cloudinary. The site loads fast, stays cheap to run, and isn't reliant on a CMS that could break or get expensive later.
