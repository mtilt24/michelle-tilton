The brief

A tax ID, a mission, and a fundraiser already on the calendar.

Two founders walked in ready to support foster youth aging out of the system, with no website, no logo, and no brand to speak of. They needed something that could walk into donor meetings, hold its own next to bigger nonprofits, and convert curious visitors into people who actually give.

What I delivered

A brand from zero, then a site to put it on.

Logo, color system, typography, voice, and photography direction, designed from scratch. Then a custom-coded multi-page website with Stripe donations and event ticketing live the day it launched. The May fundraiser ran through it without a hitch, and inquiries tripled in the first month.
