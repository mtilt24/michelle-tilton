Advancing Communities Forward

A Sacramento nonprofit empowering foster youth ages 18 to 21 with safe transitional housing, life skills, and wraparound support. Full website, brand identity, logo, and ongoing strategy.

Type Brand Identity + Custom Site
Platform Custom HTML / CSS / JS
Tech GSAP, Cloudinary, Stripe, Netlify
Year 2026
View Live Site →

ACF came to me with a mission, two passionate founders, a tax ID, and not much else. No website, no brand guide, no visual identity, and a fundraising event already on the calendar. We had to build everything from scratch.

The work spanned brand identity, logo design, copy, and a fully custom multi-page website. The brief was to feel warm, hopeful, and competent — not pity-driven. Every photo, every headline, every CTA had to lead with outcomes for the youth ACF serves, not the trauma they came from.

The site went live before the May fundraiser, with an integrated Stripe donation flow and event ticket purchase, both built directly into the custom code so the founders could accept money without paying for an extra platform.

A site that does the talking when the founders can't.

The launch lined up with the inaugural fundraising event. Donations and ticket purchases ran through the live Stripe integration without a hitch. The brand identity, originally designed for the website, has since been used across event materials, social media, and printed collateral, including the founders' first physical donor packets.

More importantly, the site makes ACF feel like the established nonprofit it's becoming, not the brand-new startup it actually is. That's exactly what the founders needed to walk into rooms with potential donors and partners.
