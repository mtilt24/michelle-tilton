Client
Aurora Vida Global
Industry
Authorized PAL Lighting distributor for Costa Rica (luxury pool and outdoor lighting)
Problem
Costa Rica's pool-lighting market was full of generic distributor sites with stock photos. They needed something that felt as elevated as the luxury properties they install in.
What I did
Logo, brand identity, copywriting, and a fully custom-coded multi-page site with product category pages and an integrated quote-request flow.
Result
A polished digital storefront that signals premium without saying it out loud. A brand instead of a brochure.
What I built

Every choice, made on purpose.

A brand from zero

Original logo, color palette in gold and deep tones, typography pairing, voice direction, and photography style. Vector deliverables and brand guidelines so the identity holds up across web, print, and proposals.

Eleven pages, every product family

Pool lighting, landscape, strip, water-feature, controllers and automation each get their own dedicated page, so architects and homeowners see exactly what they're getting before they ask for a quote.

Quotes straight to the inbox

A custom quote-request form that pushes inquiries to both the team's email and a tracked Google Sheet, no third-party CRM, no paid integrations. Every lead is captured and easy to follow up on.

Premium without saying premium

Hero video, generous photography, an unhurried scroll, and gold-on-deep imagery. The site behaves like a brand a luxury homeowner would feel comfortable buying from sight unseen, not a brochure.

English & Spanish, one click away

Most of Aurora Vida's clients live and work between two languages, so the whole site can switch from English to Spanish with a single tap. A custom-styled language toggle keeps the experience feeling like the rest of the brand, not a generic translator widget bolted on.

Built for the climate, on the phone

Architects and contractors are usually on a job site, on a phone, in the sun. The site is fast, mobile-first, and stays readable in bright daylight, so a quote can get sent without anyone needing to head back to a desk.
