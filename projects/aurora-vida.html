Aurora Vida Global is the authorized PAL Lighting distributor for Costa Rica. They sell premium underwater pool lights, landscape fixtures, strip lighting, water-feature lighting, and the controllers and automation that tie it all together — primarily to luxury homeowners, builders, and resort developers.

The brief was to create something that felt as elevated as the properties they install in. Costa Rica's pool-lighting market is full of generic distributor sites with stock photos and zero personality. Aurora Vida wanted to look like the kind of company a luxury homeowner would feel comfortable buying from sight unseen.

The work spanned the entire identity: logo design, brand system, copywriting, and a fully custom-coded website with product category pages, a gallery, and an integrated quote-request form.

A polished digital storefront that lets the product sell itself.

The site became the central point of contact for Aurora Vida's distribution business. Architects, builders, and homeowners land on a product page, see exactly what they're getting, and submit a quote request that lands directly in the team's inbox and a tracked Google Sheet. The brand system — gold and deep tones, confident typography, generous photography — signals premium without saying it out loud.

Design choices were small but deliberate: the gold logo against deep imagery, the unhurried scroll, the way each product family gets its own dedicated page rather than being lumped into a single catalog. The site behaves like a brand, not a brochure.
