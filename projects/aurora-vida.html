The work

A premium brand for a premium product, in two languages.

Aurora Vida is the authorized PAL Lighting distributor for Costa Rica, working with architects and luxury homebuilders across the country. The market was full of generic distributor sites with stock photos. They needed something that felt as elevated as the properties they light.

The work started from zero: logo, color system in gold and deep green, typography pairing, voice, and photography direction. From there, eleven custom-coded pages covering every PAL product family, an English/Spanish language toggle styled to feel like the rest of the brand, and a quote-request form that pushes inquiries straight to the team's inbox and a tracked Google Sheet.
