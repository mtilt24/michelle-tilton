Live Like Keira Foundation

Heartfelt brand identity for a 501(c)(3) nonprofit honoring Keira Bradley's legacy. Compassionate purple palette, Poppins type system, and a brand voice that always leads with action over grief.

Type Brand Guide
Year 2026

01 / Color Palette

Purple-forward with clean, high-contrast foundations.

The accent purple is the heart of the brand, representing Keira's spirit. It's paired with black and white for clarity and a light lavender for softer backgrounds. The dark accent grounds the palette for headers and footers.

02 / Typography

Poppins throughout. Modern, friendly, and highly readable.

Poppins is used for both headings and body text, keeping the brand feeling unified and approachable. Heavier weights for headings create strong visual hierarchy, while lighter weights keep body text easy to read.

03 / Buttons & UI

Clear, purposeful calls to action.

Primary buttons use Keira Purple for donation and engagement CTAs. The dark accent provides an alternative for navigation and footer actions. All buttons use Poppins Medium 500 with 5px border-radius.

04 / Brand Voice

Compassionate, action-driven, and forward-looking.

Live Like Keira speaks with heart and purpose. The voice is warm but always moving toward action. Center the mission and the impact, not the grief. Every message should inspire people to show up for others.

Do

"On her last day, Keira was doing what she loved, shopping for Christmas gifts for foster children. We carry that forward."

Do

"Every purchase supports camp experiences and holiday gifts for children who need it most."

Don't

"This is a tragic story and we need your help because everything is terrible."

Don't

"You HAVE to donate right now or you don't care about kids!!!"

05 / Photography

Authentic moments, warm light, community connection.

Photography should capture genuine moments of connection, kindness, and community. Favor warm, natural light and candid expressions over posed studio shots. Images should feel hopeful and joyful.

Event photography is key. Capture volunteers, participants, and supporters in action. Always prioritize images that reflect Keira's spirit of generosity and togetherness.
