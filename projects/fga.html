The work

One digital home for a podcast, a Substack, and a retreat practice.

Becky's audience was growing across a podcast, paid writing on Substack, and retreats, but everything lived on different platforms with no single place to send people. The site needed to hold space for grief and quiet curiosity, while still leading new listeners toward the next episode or trip.

Built a brand system around her existing logo: color palette, typography pairing, and voice direction. Then a fully custom-coded 10-page site that pulls Buzzsprout, Substack, Stripe, and Apple/Spotify/YouTube into one editorial experience. Set up her Mailchimp and Stripe accounts from scratch, so the back-end was already running the day the site launched.
