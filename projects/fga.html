Client
Becky Bradley, FEEL Good Always
Industry
Spiritual podcast, writing, and retreats
Problem
A growing brand with a podcast, retreats, and a Substack, all scattered across platforms. No single digital home that could hold space for grief and convert curious listeners.
What I did
Brand system around the existing logo, copywriting collaboration, and a fully custom-coded 10-page site with podcast, Substack, and Stripe integrations.
Result
A unified digital home where listeners discover the podcast, readers subscribe to writing, and seekers explore retreats. Becky owns the code, the domain, and the audience.
What I built

Every choice, made on purpose.

Sacred without being precious

A 10-page site with soft scroll animations and image-led editorial layouts. The tone holds space for someone grieving a loss while still leading curious newcomers gently toward a retreat or an episode.

A brand system around the existing logo

Color palette (plum, mauve, blush, lavender, cream), typography pairing, and voice direction so every page feels like one coherent body of work, not a collection of disconnected platforms.

Every offering, one site

Podcast (Buzzsprout), paid writing (Substack), retreat deposits (Stripe), and Apple/Spotify/YouTube embeds woven into a single experience. One place to discover everything Becky offers.

Becky owns it all

Hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain. No CMS to maintain, no monthly platform fees, no plugin updates breaking pages. Independent creator, independent infrastructure.
