Live Like Keira

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit honoring Keira Bradley's legacy by supporting foster youth and families in need. Squarespace site with donations, merchandise store, events, and community storytelling.

Client
Live Like Keira (Keira Bradley Foundation)
Industry
501(c)(3) nonprofit supporting foster youth and families in need
Problem
A meaningful mission with no online home. The foundation needed a place to accept donations, sell merchandise, list events, and tell Keira's story.
What I did
Squarespace build, donation portal setup, e-commerce store, event listings, copywriting, and ongoing maintenance.
Result
A heartfelt, functional hub honoring Keira's legacy where supporters can donate, shop, and get involved in one place.
What I built

Every choice, made on purpose.

Donations, the easy way

An integrated donation portal with one-time and recurring options, plus tax receipt automation, so the foundation can focus on the mission instead of the paperwork.

Merch that funds the mission

A full e-commerce setup for apparel, stickers, and accessories with branded fulfillment, so every sale flows directly into supporting foster youth and families in need.

Events that fill up

Event listings, signups, and fundraiser promotion (including the Costa Rica giveaway) all in one place, easy for the team to update and easy for supporters to find.

Keira's story, told the way it deserves

Visual storytelling that honors Keira's legacy and makes the mission feel personal, so first-time visitors leave knowing exactly why and how to help.
