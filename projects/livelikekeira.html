The work

One online home for the mission, the merch, and the memory.

The Keira Bradley Foundation had a meaningful mission and no online home. The team needed somewhere to accept donations, sell merchandise, list events, and tell Keira's story in a way that felt personal, not transactional.

Built inside Squarespace with custom styling, a Stripe-powered donation portal, an e-commerce setup for apparel and accessories, and event listings the team can update themselves. Set up the foundation's Stripe and Mailchimp accounts from scratch and walked the team through the back-end, so donations, tickets, and email signups all worked from day one.
