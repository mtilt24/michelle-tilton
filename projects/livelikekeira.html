Live Like Keira

Type Website Design & Build
Platform Squarespace
Year 2026
View Live Site

Overview

The Keira Bradley Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to honoring Keira's legacy by supporting children and families in need. On her last day, Keira was doing what she loved, shopping for Christmas gifts for foster children. The foundation carries that mission forward.

I built the entire Squarespace site to serve as the foundation's digital home, handling donations, event promotion, merchandise sales, and community storytelling.

What I Built

Donation System

Integrated donation portal making it easy for supporters to contribute directly to the foundation's initiatives.

E-commerce Store

Merchandise store featuring apparel, stickers, and accessories with the foundation's branding to fund the mission.

Events & Community

Event listings, community gatherings, and fundraising activities with Costa Rica giveaway promotions.

Storytelling

Keira's story told through compelling imagery and copy that connects emotionally with visitors and drives action.

The Result

A heartfelt, functional nonprofit website that honors Keira's legacy while making it simple for supporters to donate, shop, and get involved. The site serves as the foundation's primary hub for community engagement and fundraising.
