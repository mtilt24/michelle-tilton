The Keira Bradley Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to honoring Keira's legacy by supporting children and families in need. On her last day, Keira was doing what she loved — shopping for Christmas gifts for foster children. The foundation carries that mission forward.

I built the Squarespace site to serve as the foundation's digital home, handling donations, event promotion, merchandise sales, and the storytelling that makes the mission feel personal. Every section was designed to honor Keira while making it simple for supporters to actually take action.
