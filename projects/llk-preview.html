Keira's Story

On Keira's last day on earth, she was doing what she loved, shopping for Christmas gifts for foster children. As a camp counselor at Camp Rockin' U starting at age 14, she dedicated herself to making a difference in the lives of children who needed it most.

The Keira Bradley Foundation carries her mission forward, channeling her passion for helping others into meaningful community initiatives.
