Our mission at NorCal pool productions:

We want to design and build you your own private outdoor getaway.

We are a complete design/build company with innovative designs and the highest quality construction from start to finish. We pride ourselves on our superior customer service. We work with you to create a unique custom design for your backyard which means, no two pools that we build are alike. All projects are owner supervised and built with care and attention to detail. You explain to us what you want, and we will build it or let Nor-Cal Pool Productions draw it out for you.

We Specialize In:

  • Custom Pools & Spas

  • Sculpted Gunite Swimming Pools

  • Landscaping

  • Concrete Work & Acid Staining

  • BBQs & Firepits

  • Outdoor Fireplaces

  • Pizza Ovens

  • Solar Heating
