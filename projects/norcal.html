What I built

Every choice, made on purpose.

A site they actually own

Built from scratch instead of dropped into a template, so the site loads fast, isn't slowed down by extra code they don't need, and doesn't cost them a monthly platform fee. They own the work and the infrastructure.

Storytelling that pulls you in

Day-to-night image transitions, scroll reveals, and a rising consultation panel keep visitors moving through the site instead of bouncing. The pools get to do the selling, the way they should.

Looks right on every screen

Designed mobile first, because most of their leads land on the site from a phone. Whether someone's at a job site or planning a backyard from the couch, the experience is intentional, not squeezed.

A brand that finally matches the work

An updated color palette, typography pairing, and photography direction so every touchpoint, from the homepage to a printed proposal, feels as premium as the pools they build.

Quote requests, straight to the inbox

The contact and consultation forms send every submission directly to the team's email the moment it's filled out. No third-party CRM to log into, no missed leads sitting in some other tool.
