Overview

Nor-Cal Pool Productions is a custom pool and backyard construction company serving Northern California with 20+ years of experience and 400+ pools built. They needed a website that matched the premium quality of their work, but the old site wasn't doing them justice.

I handled the full rebrand and built a completely custom-coded website from scratch. No templates, no page builders. 100% hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The Challenge

The existing website didn't reflect the caliber of Nor-Cal's work. It lacked visual impact, had no clear brand identity, and wasn't converting visitors into consultations. The owner needed a site that positioned them as the premium choice in Northern California pool construction.

The Rebrand

I developed a complete new brand identity including updated color palette, typography system, and visual direction. The goal was to move from generic contractor vibes to a polished, premium feel that reflects the quality of their custom builds.

View Brand Guide

What I Built

Custom Code

100% hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No templates, no page builders, no platform limitations.

Scroll Animations

Custom GSAP animations including a day/night image crossfade, section reveals, and a rising CTA section that animates into view.

Responsive Design

Fully responsive from mobile to desktop with a fixed navigation, blur effects, and optimized image loading via Cloudinary.

Performance

No framework bloat. Fast load times, optimized assets, and clean semantic HTML for SEO.

The Result

A premium, high-performance website that positions Nor-Cal Pool Productions as the top custom pool builder in Northern California. The site drives consultation requests and showcases their portfolio in a way that matches the quality of their actual work.
