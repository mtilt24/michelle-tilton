Nor-Cal Pool Productions is a custom pool and backyard construction company serving Northern California with 20+ years of experience and 400+ pools built. They needed a website that matched the premium quality of their work, but the old site wasn't doing them justice.

I handled the full rebrand and built a completely custom-coded website from scratch. No Squarespace, no Webflow, no page builders. The result is fast, distinct, and feels like the brand actually owns its corner of the market.

The challenge

The site didn't match the work.

The existing website was a Squarespace template that didn't reflect the caliber of Nor-Cal's actual builds. No clear brand identity, low visual impact, and the site wasn't converting visitors into consultation requests. The owner wanted to position the company as the premium choice in Northern California pool construction — which meant the site needed to actually look the part.

The new Nor-Cal site positions the company as the premium custom pool builder in Northern California. The portfolio gallery, scroll storytelling, and clear consultation CTAs turn visits into consults — the entire point of the rebuild.
