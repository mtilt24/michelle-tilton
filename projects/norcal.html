The work

A custom-built site that finally matches the caliber of the pools.

Nor-Cal had been running on a Squarespace template that didn't reflect the level of work they were doing. Photos of premium custom builds were sitting inside a layout that looked like every other pool company. Conversions were weak, the brand felt generic, and the site couldn't compete with how they wanted to be perceived.

The work covered a brand refresh, photography direction, copywriting, and a fully custom-coded site, hand-built in HTML, CSS, and JS. Day-to-night image transitions, scroll-triggered reveals, and a rising consultation panel keep visitors moving through the site, with quote requests landing straight in the team's inbox.
