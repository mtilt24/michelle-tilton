Hair Salon Redesign

Repositioning a hair stylist's website from generic salon to premium curly hair specialist. Designed end-to-end in Photoshop before development handoff.

TypeWebsite Redesign
ToolsPhotoshop
Year2026

A website redesign for a hair stylist who wanted to reposition her brand as more upscale, with a stronger focus on curly hair cuts as her specialty. The full redesign was designed in Photoshop to visualize the new direction before any development started.

The brief was to move from generic salon vibes (could be any hairdresser) to a confident, premium curly-hair specialist. Visual hierarchy, photography, and copy all needed to shift to support the new positioning.

What I did

Brand repositioning, art direction, full Photoshop redesign mockups, copywriting.

Specialty

Premium curly-hair cuts and color.

A polished, elevated direction.

The redesign shifts the brand positioning from generic salon to premium curly-hair specialist. Visual hierarchy emphasizes expertise, builds trust, and creates a more sophisticated first impression for prospective clients.
