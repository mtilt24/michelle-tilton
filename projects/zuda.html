Zuda Yoga

Squarespace site with extensive custom CSS for a premier power vinyasa studio in Placer County. Membership signups, class scheduling, workshop listings, and Costa Rica retreat booking, all in one digital home.

Zuda Yoga is a premier power vinyasa studio with locations in Rocklin and Auburn, serving the greater Placer County area. They needed a website that reflected their warm, community-driven brand while handling membership signups, class scheduling, workshop listings, and retreat bookings.

Built on Squarespace with heavy custom CSS to push beyond template limitations and create a design that doesn't look or feel like a template. The platform stays maintainable for the studio team; the design feels completely unique to their brand.
