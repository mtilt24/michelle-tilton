The work

A Squarespace site that finally feels like the studio.

Zuda runs two studios in Placer County with a strong, warm community, but the original site was generic, didn't surface what members were actually coming for, and wasn't ranking. Memberships, workshops, retreats, and the class schedule were all buried.

The work covered an extensive custom-CSS rebuild on top of Squarespace, page architecture, membership and event flows, copy that reflects the actual energy of the studio, and on-page SEO. CTR climbed from 1.5% to 3.9% in Search Console, and average search position moved from 19 to 4.

What's on the site

A digital home for everything Zuda offers.

Class scheduleMindbody schedule and booking widgets embedded so live class times stay in sync.
MembershipsThree-tier pricing (Intro, Monthly, Annual) with a clean signup flow.
Workshops & retreatsWorkshop calendar, Costa Rica retreat listing, and event detail pages.
SEOOn-page work moved CTR from 1.5% to 3.9% and search position from 19 to 4.
Custom CSSHeavy styling on top of Squarespace so the site doesn't read as a stock template.
Self-managedBuilt so the studio team can update events, blog posts, and classes themselves.
