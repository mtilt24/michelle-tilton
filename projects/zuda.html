Zuda Yoga

Squarespace site with extensive custom CSS for a premier power vinyasa studio in Placer County. Membership signups, class scheduling, workshop listings, and Costa Rica retreat booking, all in one digital home.

TypeWebsite Design & Build
PlatformSquarespace + Custom CSS
Year2026
View Live Site →
Client
Zuda Yoga, two studios in Rocklin and Auburn, CA
Industry
Premier power vinyasa yoga studio and community
Problem
A site that didn't reflect the studio's warm community brand or surface what members actually came for: memberships, workshops, retreats, and class schedules.
What I did
Squarespace build with extensive custom CSS, page architecture, membership and event flows, on-page SEO and content.
Result
CTR went from 1.5% to 3.9% and average search position climbed from 19 to 4. Workshops and retreats now flow through a polished digital home.
What I built

Every choice, made on purpose.

Squarespace, but it doesn't look like Squarespace

Heavy custom CSS layered on top of the platform so the site feels distinctive and on-brand, while staying maintainable for the studio team to update on their own.

Memberships built to convert

Three-tier pricing (Intro, Monthly, Annual) with clear perks and signup flow. Visitors see exactly what they get, what it costs, and how to start, no friction between curiosity and commitment.

Workshops and retreats in one place

Workshop calendar, Costa Rica retreat listing, and event detail pages so the studio's offerings beyond drop-in classes get the visibility (and bookings) they deserve.

SEO that finally pays off

On-page SEO and content work moved their average Google search position from 19 to 4, and CTR from 1.5% to 3.9% in Search Console. More of the right students are finding the studio.

Class schedule, always current

Mindbody schedule and booking widgets embedded directly into the site, so the live class times, teachers, and signups stay in sync with what the studio is already running, no double-entry.

Words that sound like the studio

Rewrote core pages so the copy reflects the actual energy of Zuda, warm, grounded, a little playful, instead of the generic yoga-studio language the original site leaned on.
