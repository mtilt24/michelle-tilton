Zuda Yoga

Type Website Design & Build
Platform Squarespace + Custom CSS
Year 2026
View Live Site

Overview

Zuda Yoga is a premier power vinyasa studio with locations in Rocklin and Auburn, serving the greater Placer County area. They needed a website that reflected their warm, community-driven brand while handling membership signups, class scheduling, workshop listings, and retreat bookings.

Built on Squarespace with extensive custom CSS to push beyond template limitations and create a design that feels completely unique to their brand.

What I Built

Custom CSS

Extensive custom styling layered on top of Squarespace to achieve a design that doesn't look or feel like a template. Custom animations, layout overrides, and responsive refinements.

Membership System

Clear presentation of three membership tiers (Intro, Monthly, Annual) with pricing, perks, and signup flows that drive conversions.

Events & Retreats

Workshop calendar, Costa Rica retreat listing, and event pages that keep the community engaged and coming back.

Community Focus

Testimonial sections, instructor profiles, and gallery showcasing the studio atmosphere. 1,500+ five-star reviews highlighted throughout.

The Result

A warm, polished website that serves as the digital home for Zuda's community. The site handles everything from first-time visitor intro offers to retreat bookings, all while maintaining the studio's welcoming, inclusive brand feel.
